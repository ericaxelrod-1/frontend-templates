Groups — the scope axis {{ summary() }}

A group is SCOPE: it decides which ROWS a person may read. It is not capability — a role decides what a person may DO, and the two are stored apart and never derived from one another. Moving a group under one of its own descendants would close a loop in the hierarchy; the database refuses that, and the refusal is shown here rather than pre-empted.
