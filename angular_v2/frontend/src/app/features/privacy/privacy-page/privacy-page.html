} How Privacy Machinery Works in v2 Substantive disclosure of erasure, retention, and tombstones
delete_forever What is Erased Immediately
  • Email Address: Overwritten with a cryptographic HMAC tombstone address (e.g., deleted-<hmac>@domain).
  • Username: Neutralized to the tombstone local part to prevent identification.
  • Password Hash: Overwritten with a CSPRNG-generated bcrypt hash of an unrecorded secret, destroying the credential while preserving verification timing.
  • Active Sessions: All refresh tokens are revoked immediately in the database (REVOKE_SESSIONS_SQL).
  • Authority: All group memberships and role capabilities are stripped (authz.revoke_subject_authority()).
  • Receipt: An automated confirmation receipt is delivered to your registered email prior to anonymisation.
security What is Retained and Why
  • Security Telemetry (11 CCR §7111): Forensic login records in login_attempts (IP address, status, timestamp) are retained to investigate brute-force attacks and abuse. The username is pseudonymised to match the tombstone.
  • Statutory Compliance Ledger (11 CCR §7101(b)): Proof that an erasure request was fulfilled is recorded in privacy_request_audits. The subject is keyed by an HMAC pepper (subject_hmac), preserving compliance evidence without PII.
  • Purge Queue Pipeline: Soft-deleted tombstones reside in the database until the dormant retention window elapses, at which time the automated purge sweeper runs.
