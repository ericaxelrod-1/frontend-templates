Legal Documentation & Operator Obligations Requirements for production compliance

The template provides cryptographic mechanisms (anonymisation tombstones, automated retention sweepers, and pseudonymised compliance audit ledgers), but it cannot specify your legal position. The operator must author a complete Privacy Policy addressing:

1. Data Controller Identification
  • Legal entity name, registered office address, and contact details.
  • Designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) or privacy response contact.
  • Supervisory authority and relevant regulatory jurisdictions (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA).
2. Categories of Data Processed
  • Identity credentials (username, email address, cryptographic password hashes).
  • Profile attributes (given and family names, voluntarily submitted).
  • Security telemetry (IP address, subnet, client user agent, failed login attempts).
  • Geographic jurisdiction markers (country, region, city from geolocation).
  • User consent records and specific processing purposes opted into.
3. Legal Bases and Processing Purposes
  • Performance of contract (account provisioning, credential authentication).
  • Legitimate interests (brute-force defense, IP reputation tracking, security auditing).
  • Statutory compliance (11 CCR §7111 forensic retention, 11 CCR §7101(b) compliance records).
  • Explicit consent (optional analytics, marketing, and third-party integrations).
4. Retention Windows and Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten)
  • Retention schedules for dormant accounts, telemetry logs, and revoked refresh tokens.
  • The immediate cryptographic erasure procedure and tombstone anonymisation.
  • Statutory survival of pseudonymised security records and audit ledgers.

The Terms of Service govern user access to the deployment. The operator must define:

1. Acceptable Use and Security Responsibilities
  • User responsibility for safeguarding credentials and multi-factor authenticators.
  • Prohibition of automated credential stuffing, penetration testing without consent, and abusive scraping.
  • Compliance with applicable international trade, export, and sanctions laws.
2. Account Suspension and Administrative Disables
  • Criteria for temporary security suspensions (e.g. repeated brute-force failure thresholds).
  • Administrative termination rights for terms violations or compliance obligations.
  • Procedures for requesting account re-enablement or contesting an operator block.
3. Warranties, Liability, and Governing Law
  • Service availability commitments and planned maintenance windows.
  • Disclaimer of express and implied warranties to the extent permitted by law.
  • Limitation of liability and damages caps.
  • Choice of governing law, venue, and mandatory dispute resolution mechanisms.
